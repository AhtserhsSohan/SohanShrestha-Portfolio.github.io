Secure network design for a 60-bed aged care home

Network design · segmentation · healthcare security

A complete network design for a residential aged care facility, built in Packet Tracer and documented end to end.

  • Separate VLANs for clinical systems, staff, residents/guests, and IoT devices (nurse call, sensors, smart TVs).
  • Firewall policy and ACLs so a compromised guest or IoT device can't reach clinical records.
  • Backup and ransomware recovery plan, plus a one-page incident checklist for night-shift staff.

Why this project: I work inside aged care homes, so I designed for how these buildings really run. That means 24/7 operation, non-technical staff on shift, and devices that can't simply be switched off.
